About

For the Music

Circuit 909 is a Columbus-based event brand built on one principle: the music comes first. We create spaces where electronic music isn't background noise — it's the reason you showed up.

We started with rooftop day parties and intimate warehouse nights, drawing together the local community and traveling artists who share the same obsession. Every event is curated with intention — the lineup, the venue, the atmosphere — all designed to put you closer to the music and the people who love it.

Columbus has a scene worth showing up for. Circuit 909 is here to make sure you don't miss it.
